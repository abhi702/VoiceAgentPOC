# Demo clinic administrative information

## Demonstration only
This is a fictional clinic. All names, slots, medication records and policies are synthetic.
Never use this demonstration for real medical care.

## Opening hours
The fictional reception desk is open Monday to Friday, 8 am to 6 pm, excluding bank holidays.
This POC does not connect to a real surgery or telephone service.

## Appointments
Available appointments must be checked using the booking system. The caller must provide
their full name and date of birth, choose a slot and explicitly confirm the repeated details.
Appointment cancellation is not implemented; contact human reception.

## Repeat prescriptions
Only existing synthetic medication can be requested. A request is not prescribing approval.
The synthetic patient is Alex Demo, date of birth 1990-01-15, with an existing Metformin record.

## Medical questions
The receptionist cannot diagnose, prescribe or determine urgency. For medical advice,
contact a qualified professional or NHS 111. In an emergency call 999.
